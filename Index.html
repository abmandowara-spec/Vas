How we work.

A fixed scope and a named partner on every engagement.

  • Scoping

    A first conversation to understand the transaction or reporting need, the deadline and who will rely on our work. You receive a written scope and fee before we begin.

  • Working papers you can follow

    Every figure traces to a source document and a stated assumption. Valuation reports set out the method, the inputs and the sensitivity of the result.

  • Review before release

    A second partner reviews every report, opinion and filing before it leaves the firm.

  • Support after delivery

    We stay available for questions from banks, investors, auditors and the tax department on work we have signed.
